# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical scope and safeguards remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct comparison, prioritization, sequencing discussion, and review in English.
- Preserve the language of existing programme maps and paper records. Keep paper titles, venue names, and named concepts in their established or official form unless a gloss is useful.
- Do not treat the language of a manuscript or venue as the required language of strategic collaboration.

## Programme vocabulary

Use the four levels consistently: `broad theme`, `problem cluster`, `specific research problem`, and `independent paper`. Distinguish `candidate`, `dependency`, `sequence`, `constraint`, `trade-off`, `commitment`, and `researcher-confirmed decision`.

## Interaction and output

Compare routes through their intellectual differences and cumulative consequences, not through publication count alone. Make model-proposed routes visibly provisional. Prefer compact tables only when several candidates need the same comparison dimensions. End with the consequential choices still owned by the researcher and return any programme artifact for explicit review.
