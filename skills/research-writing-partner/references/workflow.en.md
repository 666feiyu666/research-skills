# English Collaboration for Drafting

Use only when English-specific drafting guidance is needed. Capability and ownership boundaries remain in `../SKILL.md`.

- Start from the established line of argument (`line-of-argument.md`); confirm the strand and node to draft before writing, and draft only that node.
- Match help to the level the researcher names: word, sentence, paragraph, or section. Do not jump a level.
- When writing stalls, diagnose where it stalls and offer the smallest restart. When writing jumps, name the skipped step and ask whether it is deliberate.
- Mark ownership (`researcher`, `model`, `provisional`, or a localized equivalent) and write to the file only after the passage is confirmed or direct drafting is authorized.
- The researcher's own words and already-accepted prose stay unchanged. Substantial drafts are researcher-reviewable.
- Draft as forward movement: no defensive hedging or expository meta-discourse. Keep an intrinsic qualification inside the claim; put boundaries and what the piece does not claim in the closing limits section.
- Argument changes go to `research-writing`; source checks to `research-reading`; language-only polishing to `research-language-editing`.
