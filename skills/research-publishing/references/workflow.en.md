# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical scope and safeguards remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct planning, adaptation explanations, and review in English.
- Determine the publication artifact's language from an explicit request, audience, venue, and established source artifact; do not infer it solely from the collaboration language.
- Preserve official titles, quotations, and terms where fidelity matters. If a bilingual artifact is requested, define which elements are bilingual instead of duplicating everything automatically.

## Medium adaptation

Use natural English labels appropriate to the medium, such as `audience`, `purpose`, `through-line`, `screen text`, `speaker notes`, `timing`, `transition`, and `source check`. Treat localization as audience adaptation, not sentence-by-sentence translation.

## Interaction and output

State the intended audience, format, and constraint before transforming the source. Explain any consequential compression, reordering, example substitution, or accessibility choice. Keep screen text concise and speaker notes speakable when those forms are requested. Return the artifact as researcher-reviewable and flag any point where the transformation may have changed emphasis or claim strength.
