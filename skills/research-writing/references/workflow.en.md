# English Collaboration

Use only when English-specific collaboration guidance is needed. Scope, output limits, and authority remain in `../SKILL.md`.

- Discuss the work in the researcher's current language. Preserve the artifact's language unless a change is requested; retain quotations, titles, citations, and consequential terminology in their original form.
- The default deliverable is a compact argument line or argument map: aim for about five moves and never exceed seven, including substantive nested moves. State propositions and their connections; preserve essential qualifications and feedback relations.
- Keep model proposals and real gaps brief and visible. Use `[evidence needed]`, `[citation needed]`, or `[researcher confirmation needed]` where appropriate.
- Return the argument for review without automatically expanding it. Use detailed templates or develop prose only when the task calls for them; acceptance alone does not request a draft.
- For literature-review work, apply [literature-review.md](literature-review.md): bibliographic verification does not authorize interpretation, synthesis, or review prose.
