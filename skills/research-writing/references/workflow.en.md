# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical scope and safeguards remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct planning, drafting discussion, revision explanation, and review in English.
- Preserve the language of an active manuscript. For a new artifact, follow the requested manuscript language; if none is specified, infer it from the designated writing materials rather than from source languages alone.
- Preserve quotations, citations, official titles, and established terminology. Mark any model translation and do not silently translate an active manuscript when the discussion language changes.
- Select `.en.md` or `.zh-CN.md` user-facing templates according to the target artifact language.

## Writing labels

Use concise status markers when needed: `[evidence needed]`, `[citation needed]`, `[page to verify]`, and `[researcher confirmation needed]`. Keep `line of argument`, `outline`, `paragraph plan`, `draft`, `researcher-supplied material`, `model proposal`, and `researcher-confirmed decision` distinct.

Use `provisional claim` and `observed draft structure` when these distinctions matter. An outline extracted from prose describes what the draft currently does; do not label it as a confirmed structure.

## Interaction and output

For literature-review work, apply the bibliographic-only boundary in `../SKILL.md` and `literature-review.md` before the general writing guidance below. Check source identity, edition information, citation markers, and reference correspondence; leave understanding, evaluation, relationships, and scholarly use entirely to the researcher. Do not draft or convert review prose, or offer substantive suggestions for later confirmation. Preserve substantive fields verbatim during explicitly requested transcription or formatting. Report unresolved locations as `[requires researcher interpretation]` when resolving them would need intellectual judgment; do not imply that an answer has been proposed. Use `[bibliographically verified]` only for metadata actually checked, never for understanding or evidentiary support.

Discuss consequential structural or rhetorical choices in plain English, but do not annotate every local sentence decision. Draft idiomatically in the target manuscript language instead of translating English syntax into it. Return substantive outlines, drafts, and revisions for researcher review, calling attention only to model additions or changes that may affect argument, evidence, uncertainty, or voice.

In collaborative drafting, present the compact line of argument in English and end that stage for researcher review before developing substantial prose. Do not turn this into a questionnaire: surface only consequential relations or decisions. Local revisions with an already clear argumentative function do not require a separate review stage.

When integrating designated material, explain which proposition it serves and why it is retained, moved, removed, or supplemented. Distinguish a researcher-confirmed relation from a low-risk writing decision and a model-proposed relation that must be reviewed before it governs drafting.
