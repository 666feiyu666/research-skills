# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile when the collaboration language is English. The canonical scope and safeguards remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct editorial diagnosis, scenario calibration, feedback discussion, and review in English.
- Preserve the manuscript's language unless revision or translation is explicitly authorized. Treat collaboration language, manuscript language, and venue language as separate decisions.
- Preserve quotations, venue terminology, reviewer wording, and official guidance in the inspected language when precision matters. Label translations or paraphrases.

## Editorial vocabulary

Use stage-appropriate terms such as `circulation draft`, `developmental review`, `venue calibration`, `peer-review simulation`, `submission readiness`, `revision priority`, `blocking issue`, and `later-stage improvement`. Do not use submission-ready standards to dismiss a document whose declared job is early circulation.

## Interaction and output

Lead with the manuscript's current scenario and the most consequential judgment. Separate problems in research content from problems in presentation. Make the evidence for editorial judgments visible and distinguish required fixes from optional improvements. Any material rewrite remains researcher-reviewable; identify changes that may alter claim strength, contribution, structure, or voice.
