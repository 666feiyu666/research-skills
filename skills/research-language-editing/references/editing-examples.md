# English Research Editing Examples

Use only the examples relevant to the passage. These teaching sentences are not evidence or material to insert into a manuscript. The skill's substantive boundaries take precedence over stylistic preferences.

## 1. Remove a nominalized construction without inventing an actor

This pair is reproduced from Rule 10 of the upstream [composition reference](https://github.com/richyuh/claude-skills/blob/main/writing-clearly-and-concisely/elements-of-style/03-elementary-principles-of-composition.md), which draws on Strunk's *The Elements of Style* (1918).

**Before:** A survey of this region was made in 1900.

**After:** This region was surveyed in 1900.

The revision keeps the date and passive voice. An active version would require knowing who conducted the survey.

## 2. Use parallel form without adding a research purpose

The remaining examples are newly written research adaptations of the upstream principles, with local boundary illustrations.

**Before:** The study compares how participants describe the archive and their uses of it.

**After:** The study compares how participants describe and use the archive.

The two objects of comparison remain the same. Do not add an explanation of why participants use the archive.

## 3. Cut padding while preserving uncertainty

**Before:** It is important to note that these accounts may reflect differences in access, although the available evidence does not establish a causal relationship.

**After:** These accounts may reflect differences in access, although the available evidence does not establish a causal relationship.

Remove the introductory padding. Retain the possibility and the stated evidential limit.

## 4. Preserve consequential ambiguity for researcher review

**Input:** The researcher discussed the interviews and the archive records, which were incomplete.

**Handling:** Flag whether incompleteness applies to the interviews, the archive records, or both. Do not silently substitute an interpretation merely to produce a smoother sentence.

Likewise, a statement that the evidence does not establish an effect must not become a statement that there is no effect.
