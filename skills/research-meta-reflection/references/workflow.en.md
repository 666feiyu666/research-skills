# English Collaboration Workflow

Use this profile only after the researcher explicitly invokes the Skill. The canonical scope and safeguards remain in `../SKILL.md`.

## Language decisions

- Conduct reflective dialogue in English unless the researcher explicitly requests another collaboration language.
- Preserve the language of existing records and quoted research material. Choose a new reflection record's language separately when requested.
- When a record is wanted, use [meta-reflection-record.en.md](meta-reflection-record.en.md) for English or [meta-reflection-record.zh-CN.md](meta-reflection-record.zh-CN.md) for Simplified Chinese.

## Reflective dialogue

Begin with the stage boundary and experience the researcher names. Ask one focused question at a time and follow the researcher's live reflective center rather than completing every possible lens. Distinguish what the researcher says from model observations and proposals. Do not turn a tentative response into a polished first-person conclusion without review.

## Record and review

Use first person only for language the researcher supplied or explicitly accepted. Put model reconstructions and suggested wording in a visibly provisional section. Return any record for the researcher to accept, revise, or reject; explicit acceptance may confirm it without requiring a cosmetic edit.
