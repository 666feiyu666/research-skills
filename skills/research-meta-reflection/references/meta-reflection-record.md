# Research Meta-Reflection Record

Use only sections that carry researcher-supplied or explicitly confirmed meaning at the present boundary, plus model observations that are visibly labeled for review. This is a reflective synthesis, not an activity log, project dashboard, artifact inventory, Skill-use record, or compulsory questionnaire.

```markdown
# 阶段性研究元反思：阶段或主题

- 研究项目：
- 反思范围：
- 阶段边界：（由研究者声明）
- 日期：
- 相关材料：（仅列真正支撑本次反思的材料）

## 这一阶段实际在研究什么

## 我的理解或问题发生了什么变化

## 文献与材料改变了什么

## 我现在能够支持什么判断

## 仍不清楚、不稳定或不能推出什么

## 研究方法带来了什么

## 我想保留、搁置、放弃或改变什么

## 我已经决定的方向

## 尚未决定的问题
```

Omit irrelevant sections. A short reflection with one genuine change and one unresolved limit is better than a completed template with invented content.

## Preserve Voice and Epistemic Status

- Use first-person claims only for the researcher's wording or a paraphrase they have confirmed.
- Do not infer “I understand,” “I have shown,” or “I will do next” from the existence of a note, analysis, outline, draft, review, or submission.
- Distinguish source claims, empirical findings, researcher interpretation, researcher self-assessment, and model observation.
- When provenance could be confused, use compact labels such as `[研究者确认]`, `[文献依据]`, `[材料依据]`, `[待核验]`, `[模型提问]`, or `[模型建议—未采纳]`.
- Preserve unresolved tensions instead of manufacturing closure.

## Keep the Record Reflective

- Reconstruct what changed and why it matters to the researcher; do not summarize every artifact.
- Record method observations only when they arise from the reflected stage.
- Record a direction only when the researcher chooses it. Keep unchosen possibilities under `尚未决定的问题` or omit them.
- Do not add Skill assignments, task queues, deadlines, status codes, current-file registries, or Skill-use logs.
- Link only supporting artifacts that materially ground the reflection.

## Researcher Review

- Treat the first synthesis or saved record as a review draft.
- Identify consequential paraphrases, connections, omissions, or model expansions that need inspection.
- Let the researcher accept it unchanged, edit it, request revision, or reject it.
- Revise the same active record after correction.
- Do not treat the record or its directions as researcher-confirmed until the researcher explicitly closes the review gate.
